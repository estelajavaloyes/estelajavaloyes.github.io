MY PERSPECTIVE

Start with
the science.

My background began in Biotechnology. At Centro de Investigación Príncipe Felipe, my final-degree research brought me into experimental neuroscience through work on neuroinflammation, extracellular vesicles and hyperammonemia.

I then moved closer to quantitative biomedical research through medical imaging. At Instituto Human Tech, Universitat Politècnica de València, I gained experience processing PET and DICOM data with SPM and MATLAB, including data quality control in a clinical research setting.

Clinical prediction brought these interests together. My MSc project on large vessel occlusion in acute ischemic stroke connected patient-level clinical data with statistical modelling, machine learning, model interpretation and a Streamlit decision-support prototype.

I am now pursuing an MSc in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence at University College Cork, building on that biomedical background with further statistical and computational training.

Alongside my studies, I am developing Dudu Growth: an early-stage multi-clinic management and analytics platform that gives me a practical setting for healthcare data, relational databases, product design and AI-assisted development.
