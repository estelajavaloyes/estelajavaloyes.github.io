Research context

My experience at CIPF sits at the biological foundation of my profile. The project concerned extracellular vesicles in an experimental model of hyperammonemia.

02 / EXPERIMENTAL NEUROSCIENCE

My contribution

I carried out my Biotechnology final-degree project through research experience at CIPF, investigating neuroinflammation in hepatic encephalopathy and hyperammonemia models.

The work involved interpreting experimental biological data and scientific literature within the conditions of the study.

03 / EXPERIMENTAL NEUROSCIENCE

Scientific perspective

Experimental research provides a useful lens for computational work: measurements are generated by a process, models simplify biological systems, and a result needs to be interpreted within the conditions that produced it.
