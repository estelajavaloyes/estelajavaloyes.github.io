03 / MEDICAL IMAGING

My contribution

I processed and analysed PET neuroimaging data from more than 60 traumatic brain injury patients using DICOM, SPM and MATLAB.

The portfolio focuses on that practical familiarity: biomedical data processing, data quality control and considering image quality before interpretation. It does not imply ownership of the study design or development of a new imaging algorithm.

04 / MEDICAL IMAGING

What this adds to my work

Medical imaging taught me to look upstream of a model. Data organisation, processing choices and image quality matter before any machine-learning method is selected.

That perspective complements my clinical prediction work: both require an understanding of how biomedical measurements are produced and what can reasonably be inferred from them.
