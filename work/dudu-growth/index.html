01 / HEALTHCARE PRODUCT

The product question

How could private clinics connect operational information with the questions they need to answer about their business?

Dudu explores that question through a software product: defining the users, information, workflows and decisions that the system should support.

The ambition is to make fragmented operational data more useful. This is a product direction, not a claim of measured commercial impact.

03 / HEALTHCARE PRODUCT

My role

I am developing Dudu as an independent product, using an AI-assisted workflow with Codex and Laravel.

  • Problem definition: identify what clinic users need the product to help them do.
  • Product design: shape behaviour, screens and workflows around those needs.
  • Data thinking: connect information requirements with the intended product experience.
  • Iteration: use AI-assisted implementation to turn ideas into software and refine the result.

This is product-building experience with AI-assisted development, rather than a claim of independent expert-level full-stack engineering.

04 / HEALTHCARE PRODUCT

From concept to software

Laravel and Livewire provide the application framework; PostgreSQL supports the relational data model; Codex supports implementation. Current work includes patient-data validation, duplicate detection and reporting across acquisition sources, appointments and attributed revenue.

Verified screenshots and a feature walkthrough can document the current product when a reviewable release is available. No fabricated dashboard, usage figures or customer results are presented here.

05 / HEALTHCARE PRODUCT

What this demonstrates

Dudu extends my experience beyond an analysis or research report. It asks how information becomes part of a working tool—and how to make product decisions while the software is still evolving.

The next milestone is to document the implemented workflows, validate them with intended users and distinguish observed outcomes from product hypotheses.
